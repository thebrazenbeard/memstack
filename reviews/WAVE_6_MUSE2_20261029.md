# Native Wave 6 — Muse 2 feasibility challenges (2026-10-29)

**Participants:** `/root/muse2_primary_w6` (manufacturer/primary source review), `/root/muse2_pipeline_w6` (open-source capture engineering), `/root/muse2_skeptic_w6` (hostile scientific falsification), with One as integrator. These are native OpenAI ChatGPT role-delegated reviews, **not** independent laboratory validation, actual Muse hardware readback or 13 BT2 local models.

## SOURCE findings

- [Muse official comparison](https://choosemuse.my.site.com/s/article/Comparing-Muse-Headbands): Muse 2 has four EEG scalp sites `TP9`, `AF7`, `AF8`, `TP10`; FPz reference; 256 Hz/12-bit EEG; motion and PPG are separate modalities.
- [BrainFlow board docs](https://brainflow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/SupportedBoards.html): `BoardIds.MUSE_2_BOARD` supports BLE on Windows and Linux. Its default `p21` streams four EEG channels plus IMU; alternative commands can stream PPG/AUX. Do not mix Muse 2 and Muse S Athena device IDs or inferred channel maps.
- [Muse SDK licensing](https://choosemuse.com/pages/developers): development access under a license; distinct commercial and formal research routes; the consumer app does not provide raw EEG export.
- [Lee et al. 2026, 30-person Muse 2 comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12972051/): Eyes-open/closed alpha patterns are detectable alongside eye, jaw and head-motion artifacts; device contact can be unstable. This does not establish autobiographical memory decoding.
- [Muse LSL source](https://github.com/alexandrebarachant/muse-lsl): a possible acquisition alternative, subject to platform/BLE and timing validation.

## Hostile verdict: REFINE

> **High:** Four frontal/temporal dry EEG channels cannot be treated as equivalent to Essex's 64 channels and do not directly read hippocampal engrams.

> **High:** Eye movements, alpha, muscle activity, prompt identity, session drift, and stimulus timing can create apparent memory-classification effects without recovery of an experienced event.

> **High:** A Bluetooth connection or raw CSV export is insufficient verification. Repeated session integrity, exact channel identities, sample gaps and timing/marker synchronization require independent readback.

> **High:** Overlapping windows, trial/session leakage, and post-response zero-phase filtering can invalidate apparent neural gain even with accurate external event labels.

## Bounded implementation order

1. Choose one local BLE acquisition path and verify host/Bluetooth support; do not install or pair devices without the relevant authorization.
2. Validate a synthetic four-channel input through the proposed pipeline, with explicit EEG allowlist, artifact labels separated and post-event impulse falsifier.
3. Conduct a short voluntary M0 eyes-open/closed, blink, jaw and stillness quality check **only after consenting to local raw neural capture and secure storage**.
4. If M0 passes across sessions, preregister a small controlled recognition/voluntary recall experiment with context-only, artifact-only and shuffled trials under day-disjoint validation.
5. Report no more than measured signal quality or bounded recall-state prediction. A true event/experience backup claim remains **UNKNOWN/unproven**.
